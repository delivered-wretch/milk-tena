A family farm in Limuru

Our family has kept dairy cattle in Limuru's cool highlands for three generations. What began with five cows is now a modern farm with milking parlour, cold chain and a small processing plant, employing 35 people from nearby villages.

We also buy surplus milk from 60 smallholder farmers around us at fair, prompt prices, supporting local livelihoods.

Questions

Is the milk pasteurised?

Yes. Our fresh milk is pasteurised and chilled the same morning. Raw milk is not sold.

How long does it keep?

Refrigerated at 4°C or below, fresh milk lasts 4 to 5 days. Boil or heat as you prefer.

Can I cancel a subscription?

Yes, anytime. Message us before 8:00 pm to pause or cancel the next day's delivery.

Do you deliver outside Nairobi?

We deliver to Kiambu, Limuru and nearby towns. For other areas, contact us for wholesale arrangements.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes. School and family farm tours run on the first Saturday of each month. Book through WhatsApp.
